War Simulator Game

First player 1 will have their turn. They will have to gather resources to create an army of soldiers to go against player 2's army that player 2 will create with the same rules after player 1 creates their army. There will be 4 different unit types: defense, melee, range, rideable. You will need materials to make different types of soldiers.

Each player will get 3 mins to gather resources. You gather resources from a 2D top down map, using WASD or arrow keys to make your player walk then you have to break resources by pressing left click on mouse (no tools needed). The map is larger than the screen and will scroll as you move around on it. Resources get replenished after player 1's turn so player 2 has the same opportunity to gather. Examples of resources that you can find are: wood, steel, wire, horses. Units are made as follows; defense requires 2 steel; melee requires 1 steel and 1 wood; range requires 1 wire and 1 wood; rideable requires 1 horse and 1 steel.

After resource gathering, the battle takes place on the same resource gathering map, but smaller so you can see the whole thing. The resources reset on the map and become obstacles. Each player has a turn to position their units. After both teams have been set up you will go into a birds eye view then you can click a battle button with left click on mouse to start the battle and see who wins. Each unit type will have an AI controlling it's movement and attacks. Defense will stand in front of other units to block attacks. Melee will run and attack in close range. Range will stay back and shoot arrows. Rideable will run faster than melee and attack close up.
